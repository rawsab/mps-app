A nutritional program sets a client's calorie targets over a number of weeks. The client logs their weight and calories each day, and you follow along in a spreadsheet.

## Creating a nutritional program

1. On the client's page, find **Programs** and choose **New Nutritional Program**. The client needs the Nutrition service first.
2. Fill in the program details below. They save on their own as you type.
3. Select **Publish**, check the summary, then select **Publish program**.

**Publish** stays greyed out until the program has a Program Style, a Start Date and both starting calorie values.

## Program details

- **Program Style**: the program's name, such as "Lean bulk". The client sees it.
- **Start Date** and **Weeks**: when the program runs, from 1 to 52 weeks. The spreadsheet's dates follow the Start Date.
- **Weight unit**: lb or kg. See Changing the weight unit below.
- **Program notes**: shown to the client.

### The starting values

- **Starting Predicted Maintenance Calories**: how many calories a day you expect keeps the client's weight steady.
- **Starting Predicted Daily Caloric Goal**: how many calories a day you want them to eat.
- **Starting Predicted Weight Change Per Week**: worked out for you from the two values above. You can't type in it.

The weight change is (goal − maintenance) × 7 ÷ 3,500, in pounds, then converted to the program's unit. For example, a goal of 2,300 with a maintenance of 2,800 gives −1 lb a week. It's an estimate.

Program details stay private until you publish. Publishing again later keeps every daily entry saved against its date. A program in draft shows **Draft · staff only**. A published one shows **Live**.

<Callout tone="note">
Programs can overlap in dates. You'll see a notice, and publishing is still allowed. Overlapping programs share the client's daily entries for the same dates.
</Callout>

## What clients log

Each day the client enters three values:

- Today's weight.
- Calories consumed yesterday.
- Caloric output yesterday.

The calories always belong to the day before. A log made on Tuesday holds Tuesday's weight and Monday's calories.

In the spreadsheet, that whole log goes on Tuesday's row. The calorie columns are called **Calories Consumed Day Before** and **Caloric Output Day Before** for this reason. Hover over a calorie cell to see which day it's for.

A value of 0 is a real zero. A blank means the client hasn't answered.

## The client spreadsheet

Open **Client spreadsheet** from a published program to see what the client has logged. Each week is its own table, one row per day. Next to the client's entries are columns the app works out:

- **Daily Caloric Balance**: calories consumed minus caloric output.
- **Average Weight**: the average of the last seven days' weigh-ins. It appears once there are at least three.

Beside each week are its **Weekly Caloric Values** and **Weekly Weight Values**: the average and weekly caloric balance, average output, and the predicted and average weight change. At the top, three totals sum up the whole program: predicted, actual and average weight change per week.

Calculated cells and future dates are greyed out and can't be edited.

## Missing entries

Cells on days up to today with no weight or calorie entry are highlighted and marked **Missing**. A notice above the spreadsheet says when there are any. The client sees the same highlights in their own spreadsheet, so they can fill them in.

## Correcting an entry

1. Select the cell and type the right number. To change the number that's there, double-click the cell, or press Enter or F2.
2. Press Enter or Tab. Your change saves straight to the client's record and is marked as a coach correction.

Corrected cells have a light gold tint.

## Working in the spreadsheet

- **Select**: click a cell, or move with the arrow keys. Hold Shift and click, drag, or use the arrow keys to select a block of cells. <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>A</Kbd> or <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>A</Kbd> selects the whole week.
- **Copy, cut and paste**: use the usual keyboard shortcuts, or right-click for the cell menu. Pasting opens **Review pasted values**, which lists each cell before and after. Select **Apply paste** to save it. Only the client's input cells change. Dates and calculated cells are kept.
- **Clear**: press Delete or Backspace, or choose **Clear input cells** from the cell menu.
- **Undo and redo**: use **Undo** and **Redo** above each week, the cell menu, or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>Z</Kbd> and <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>Y</Kbd> (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>Z</Kbd> and <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> <Kbd>Z</Kbd> on a Mac). Undo saves the earlier value back to the client's record.
- **Escape** cancels what you're typing in a cell.

If an entry couldn't be saved, you'll see **Entries not saved**. Select **Retry save** to try again, or **Discard unsaved edits** to drop it. Leaving the page with unsaved entries asks you first.

If the client changed the same cells since you opened the page, Undo stops and asks you to reload.

## Correction history

Open **Correction history** below the spreadsheet to see the latest 100 changes to saved entries, with the value before and after each one. Each change is marked **Coach correction** or **Client correction**. Calorie changes are listed by the day the calories were eaten.

## Trends

The **Trends** chart shows one value over the **Last 7 Days** or **Last 30 Days**. Choose it under **Data**: Weight, Calories consumed, Caloric output or Daily caloric balance. Hover over or move to a point to see its value.

Days without an entry are left out, never counted as zero. The chart needs at least two entries in the range. Clients see the same chart.

## Changing the weight unit

Change **Weight unit** in the program details. Weights in the spreadsheet, averages, weekly values and predictions switch to the new unit straight away. The client sees the new unit once you publish.

The client's saved weigh-ins aren't rewritten. Each one keeps the unit it was entered in, and is converted for display. Changing the unit clears the spreadsheet's Undo history.

## Archived programs

An archived nutritional program is read-only. Its spreadsheet and history are kept.
