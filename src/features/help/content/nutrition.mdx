A nutritional program sets a client's calorie targets over a number of weeks. The client logs their weight and calories each day, and you follow along in a spreadsheet.

## Creating a nutritional program

On the client's page, find **Programs** and choose **New Nutritional Program**. The client needs the Nutrition service first.

## Program details

- **Start Date** and **Weeks**: when the program runs. The spreadsheet's dates follow the Start Date.
- **Weight unit**: kilograms or pounds for this program.
- **Starting Predicted Maintenance Calories**, **Starting Predicted Daily Caloric Goal** and **Starting Predicted Weight Change Per Week**: your starting targets.
- **Program notes**: shown to the client.

Program details stay private until you publish. Select **Publish program** to share them with the client. Publishing again later keeps every daily entry saved against its date.

<Callout tone="note">
Programs can overlap in dates. You'll see a notice, and publishing is still allowed.
</Callout>

## What clients log

Each day the client enters three values:

- Today's weight.
- Calories consumed yesterday.
- Caloric output yesterday.

The calories always belong to the day before. A log made on Tuesday holds Tuesday's weight and Monday's calories.

In the spreadsheet, that whole log goes on Tuesday's row. The calorie columns are called **Calories Consumed Day Before** and **Caloric Output Day Before** for this reason. Hover over a calorie cell to see which day it's for.

<NutritionDemo name="nutrition-week" />

A value of 0 is a real zero. A blank means the client hasn't answered.

## The client spreadsheet

Each week is its own table, one row per day. Next to the client's entries are columns the app works out:

- **Daily Caloric Balance**: calories consumed minus caloric output.
- **Average Weight**: the average of the last seven days' weigh-ins. It appears once there are at least three.

Each week also shows its weekly caloric balance, average output and predicted and actual weight change. Calculated cells and future dates can't be edited.

Highlighted cells marked **Missing** are days up to today with no weight or calorie entry.

## Correcting an entry

1. Select the cell and type the right number.
2. Press Enter. Your change saves straight to the client's record and is marked as a coach correction.

Corrected cells have a light gold tint.

## Archived programs

An archived nutritional program is read-only. Its spreadsheet and history are kept.
