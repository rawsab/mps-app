## Personal details

Set your preferred name, phone number and date of birth. Your email is your sign-in email. Ask an administrator if it needs to change.

## Profile picture

Upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB. It shows in a circle in the sidebar and to your clients.

## Time zone

Sessions you schedule use this time zone. Clients see each session in their own time zone.

## Personal training sessions

- **Meeting link**: the video call link clients see on every session. Leave it blank if you don't have one yet.
- **Late cancellation window**: a client cancellation sent with less notice than this many hours is marked late.

## Availability

Availability decides when clients can pick times for sessions.

- **Working hours**: your usual week. Days without hours are unavailable.
- **Notice**: the soonest a client can pick, in hours.
- **Look ahead**: how far ahead clients can pick, in days.
- **Gap**: minutes kept free before and after each meeting.

<Callout tone="note">
If you add no working hours, clients can't pick new times. For a holiday or a one-off change, use Time off and extra hours on the Meetings page instead of changing your week.
</Callout>

## Payments

Your **e-transfer email** is shown to clients you are the primary coach for when they pay you. It can be different from your sign-in email. Leave it blank if you don't take e-transfers.
