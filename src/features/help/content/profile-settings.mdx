Each section saves on its own. Change what you need, then select that section's Save button.

## Setting up for the first time

1. Upload a **Profile picture**.
2. Check your **Time zone**, then select **Save time zone**.
3. Add your **Meeting link**, then select **Save session settings**.
4. Set your working hours under **Availability**, then select **Save availability**.
5. Add your **E-transfer email**, then select **Save e-transfer email**.

Once the meeting link and e-transfer email are saved, Setup required goes away.

## Profile picture

Select **Upload picture** and choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB. It saves straight away. It shows in a circle in the sidebar and to your clients.

To swap it, select **Change picture**. **Remove picture** puts back the plain outline of a person.

## Personal details

Set your **Preferred name**, **Date of birth** and **Phone number**, then select **Save profile**. Include the country code in your phone number if needed.

Your **Email** is your sign-in email and can't be changed here. Ask an administrator if it needs to change.

## Time zone

Sessions you schedule, your working hours and your time off use this time zone. Clients see each session in their own time zone.

If this device is set to a different time zone, the page says so. Select **Use it** to switch to the device's time zone.

## Personal training sessions

- **Meeting link**: the video call link clients see on every session. Use a full link starting with https://. Leave it blank if you don't have one yet.
- **Late cancellation window (hours)**: a client cancellation of a personal training session sent with less notice than this is marked late. The default is 24 hours.

Select **Save session settings** to save both.

## Availability

The Availability card sets your working hours and booking rules. Together with your time off, they decide which times clients can pick when they ask to move a meeting. Select **Save availability** to save them.

See [Availability and open times](/coach/help/availability) for how to set your hours and what each rule does.

<Callout tone="note">
For a holiday or a one-off change, use Time off & extra hours on the Meetings page instead of changing your week.
</Callout>

## Payments

Your **E-transfer email** is shown to clients you are the primary coach for when they pay you. It can be different from your sign-in email. Leave it blank if you don't take e-transfers. Select **Save e-transfer email** to save it.
