Clients have their own app. It shows only their own records, and only what you have published or shared. This page explains what they see, so you can guide them.

## Signing in

Clients can't sign up on their own. An administrator sends an invitation from the client's page, and the client chooses a password from the link in the email. Until they first sign in, the client shows as Invited.

If a client's access is disabled, they can't see anything, even if they are already signed in.

## Their tabs

The tabs follow the client's services. A tab for a service they don't have is hidden. A client with two or more services sees one **Services** tab instead. On a computer it opens a list of their services. On a phone it opens a page with each service and its programs, with the current program marked **Current**.

| Tab | Shown when | What's on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Always | Their billing, next meeting, services, today's to-dos and progress. |
| Mesocycle | They have Mesocycle | Their published mesocycle programs, for logging workouts. |
| Personal training | They have Personal training | Their sessions, program and attendance figures. On phones the tab says PT. |
| Nutrition | They have Nutrition | Their nutritional program and daily log. |
| Progress | They have any service | Summaries of their nutritional programs. |
| Meetings | Always | Every meeting in the next 60 days, with Reschedule and Cancel meeting. |
| Billing | Always | Their balance, statements, prices and payments, with Pay. |

Their picture at the top opens their Profile settings, where they set their name, picture and time zone. Session times and daily logs use that time zone.

## Home

Home greets the client by first name and shows:

- **Billing**: what they owe, with **Pay**, or Paid up.
- **Next meeting**: the time, the meeting link and the program it follows. **All meetings** opens a list of every meeting in the next 60 days.
- **Your services**: links to each service, and **Request a new service**.
- **To-dos**: the daily nutrition entry and their next workout.
- **Progress**: a quick look at their training and nutrition.

## Programs

Clients see a program only after you publish it. Drafts and unpublished changes stay private. The program marked **Current** is the one they follow.

Archived programs stay visible to the client, read only. They're listed under **Archived programs**, below the other programs on the Mesocycle page and in Your program on the Personal training page. The list starts closed.

Each program opens on its weeks and days. A switch at the top shows it two ways:

- **Workout**: one day at a time, each exercise with its targets, notes and demo links.
- **Spreadsheet**: the whole week as a table, like yours.

Program notes and a progress bar show above the workouts. Resources are listed below them.

To see a published program as the client does, open it in the editor and select **Client log**.

## Logging workouts

For a mesocycle, the client starts a day, enters the weight and reps for each set, and selects **Mark workout complete**. After a workout they can score Stimulus and Fatigue. See [Mesocycle](/coach/help/mesocycle) for the details.

Personal training programs are read only. The client follows along in your sessions, and you record attendance.

<Callout tone="note">
A workout the client has started keeps the targets it began with. Publishing a new version doesn't change it.
</Callout>

## Logging nutrition

Each morning the client logs today's weight, and yesterday's calories consumed and caloric output. Their log fills one row of your nutrition spreadsheet. Their spreadsheet highlights days up to today with nothing logged as **Missing**, as yours does, so they can fill them in. See [Nutrition](/coach/help/nutrition).

## Meetings and requests

The client sees their next meeting on Home, in their own time zone. **All meetings** on Home opens their Meetings page, which lists every meeting in the next 60 days by day, across all their services. Cancelled meetings stay on the list, crossed out.

While a meeting is scheduled and hasn't started, they can ask to change it, from Home or from the Meetings page.

- **Reschedule**: they pick a day, then one of your open times. If none work, they tick **None of these work** and write which times suit them.
- **Cancel meeting**: they can add a reason.

Nothing changes until you answer. A request inside your late cancellation window warns them first. They can withdraw a request while it is waiting. Each waiting request shows on its meeting. Your answer shows on Home for a week.

On their Personal training page, each past session shows the shared notes and resources you added. Private notes never show.

## Billing and paying

The client's Billing page shows their balance, monthly statements, their prices and the payments you have recorded.

1. They select **Pay** and see the amount and your e-Transfer email.
2. They send the e-Transfer from their bank.
3. They select **I've sent it**, or **I've paid this** on one charge or month, and enter the amount and date.

This sends you a notice. Their balance changes only after you record the payment. See [Billing](/coach/help/billing).

## What clients never see

- Notes you keep about the client, and private notes on meetings, patterns, payments and credits.
- Reasons you give when correcting attendance.
- Your time off, extra hours and booking rules. They only see the times that are open.
- Other clients, and other coaches' calendars.
