Meetings covers personal training sessions and check-ins. You schedule them from a client's page and follow them on the Meetings page.

## Before you start

Set these in Profile settings first:

- Your **time zone**. Meetings are scheduled in the primary coach's time zone. Clients see them in their own.
- Your **Meeting link**, if you meet by video. Clients see it on every meeting.
- Your **Late cancellation window**, under Personal training sessions.
- Your **Availability**: the times clients can pick when they ask to reschedule. See Availability and open times in Help.

A client needs an active service and a primary coach before you can schedule for them.

## Adding one meeting

1. Open the client's page and find **Meetings**.
2. Select **Add meeting**.
3. Choose the **Date**, **Start** and length.
4. Under **About**, choose what the meeting is for. You can pick more than one service.
5. Choose a program for each service, or leave it on **Current program**.
6. Add private notes if you like, then select **Add meeting**.

Only meetings about **Personal training** are billed. A personal training meeting can be **Solo** or **Partner**. Partner sessions use the partner price and are charged to this client.

## Repeating meetings

Most clients train at the same times each week. A repeating pattern creates those meetings for you.

1. On the client's Meetings card, select **Repeat meetings**.
2. Choose what the meetings are about, as for one meeting.
3. Choose how often it **Repeats**: every week, or every 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
4. Choose **Starts on**, and **Ends on** if the pattern should stop on a date.
5. Under **Days**, select each day. Set its **Start** and **Minutes**. A new day copies the last day's time and length.
6. Check **Next meetings**, which lists the first dates the pattern makes.
7. Select **Set up meetings**.

### Changing a pattern

You can change every meeting from a date on, or one meeting only.

- **From a date on**: select the pencil on the pattern, or open a meeting and choose **change the repeating pattern**. Set **Apply from** to the first date that changes. Earlier meetings stay as they are.
- **One meeting only**: open the meeting and use **Change this meeting only**. It is then marked **Changed from the pattern**. Later changes to the pattern keep it as it is.

### Pattern notes

Select **Notes** on the pattern to add notes that show on every meeting in it. They are private to staff. Each meeting can also have its own notes.

### Ending a pattern

1. Select **End** on the pattern.
2. Choose **No meetings from**: the first date with no meetings.
3. Select **Stop repeating**.

Meetings before that date stay. Meetings you changed, or that have notes, resources, attendance or client requests, stay as they are.

### Paused patterns

A pattern shows **Paused** while none of its services are active. It makes no new meetings until a service is resumed. Resuming doesn't fill in the meetings it missed. If every service is paused or ended, the Meetings card offers to cancel the remaining meetings.

## The client's Meetings card

- **Next 31 days** lists upcoming meetings. Each says what it is about and whether it is Repeating, Changed or One-off.
- **Past** lists the latest five. **View all past meetings** opens the full history.
- **Hide cancelled** hides cancelled meetings from the list.

## The Meetings page

The Meetings page shows meetings for all your clients.

- **To-dos**: requests from clients to move or cancel a meeting.
- **Record attendance**: past meetings that still need attendance.
- **Next meeting** and **Upcoming**: what's coming next.
- **Time off & extra hours**: changes to your usual week. See Availability and open times in Help.
- **Calendar**: your meetings by **Month**, **Week** or **Day**. **Today** and the arrows move through dates. Select a day in month view to open it. Time outside your availability is striped. **Hide cancelled** works here too.

## Inside a meeting

Select any meeting to open it. Its badges show its status, and **Not billed** if it isn't personal training. From there you can:

- Change its date, time, topic or program under **Change this meeting only**, then select **Save meeting**.
- Select **Cancel meeting**, with an optional reason the client sees. A cancellation by staff is never late.
- **Reinstate meeting** if it was cancelled and hasn't started.
- Write **Shared with client** notes, which the client sees with the meeting.
- Write private notes, under **Private to staff**.
- Add **Resources**: images, videos and PDFs up to 50 MB with **Upload file**, or **Add link** for longer videos. Resources are always shared with the client.

If you leave a service on **Current program**, the meeting follows whatever program is current for it.

## Client requests

Clients can ask to reschedule or cancel. Requests appear in To-dos and in Activity, and the meeting shows **Reschedule requested** or **Cancellation requested**.

1. Open the meeting. The request is at the top, with the client's message and any time they picked.
2. For a reschedule, check the new date and time, then select **Move meeting**.
3. For a cancellation, select **Accept cancellation**. If it came in inside your late cancellation window, it is recorded as a late cancellation.
4. Or select **Decline** to keep the meeting as it was.

You can add a **Reply** with either answer. The client sees it.

Once a meeting has started, its request can only be declined. Answer a request before recording attendance.

## Recording attendance

Record attendance after each session. It counts toward the client's progress, and it charges personal training sessions.

Under **Record attendance** on the Meetings page:

1. Select the tick if the client came, or the cross for a no-show.
2. The meeting leaves the list, and a notice confirms it.
3. If you chose the wrong one, select **Undo** in the notice. The meeting comes back with nothing recorded.

<AttendanceDemo />

**Mark all completed** records every listed meeting as attended. Mark any no-shows first. It can't be undone from the notice, but each meeting can still be corrected.

To correct attendance later, open the meeting and change its **Status** to Attended, No-show or Not recorded. You can give a reason. It is kept with the change, and the client doesn't see it.
