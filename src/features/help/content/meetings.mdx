Meetings covers personal training sessions and check-ins. You schedule them from a client's page and manage them on the Meetings page.

## Before you start

Set these in Profile settings first:

- Your **time zone**.
- Your **meeting link**, if you meet by video.
- Your **availability**: the hours clients can pick when they ask to move a session.
- Your **late cancellation window**.

## Scheduling a meeting

Open the client's page and find **Meetings**.

- **Add meeting** schedules a single meeting.
- **Repeat meetings** sets up a repeating pattern: choose the days, a start time for each, the start date and an optional end date.

Each meeting says what it is about, such as personal training or a nutrition check-in. A personal training session can be **Solo** or **Partner**. Partner sessions use the partner price and are charged to this client.

To end a pattern, choose **Stop repeating** and pick the first date with no meetings. Meetings you changed, or that have notes, resources, attendance or requests, stay as they are.

## The Meetings page

- **Calendar**: your meetings by month, week or day. Striped times are outside your availability.
- **Upcoming**: your next meetings in a list.
- **To-dos**: requests from clients to move or cancel a session.
- **Record attendance**: past meetings that still need attendance.
- **Time off and extra hours**: changes to your usual week.

## Inside a meeting

Select any meeting to open it. From there you can:

- Move it to another date or time.
- Cancel it, or reinstate a cancelled one.
- Add **private notes**, which only staff see.
- Add **shared notes**, which the client sees with the meeting.
- Add **resources**: images, videos and PDFs up to 50 MB, or links for longer videos. Resources are always shared with the client.

## Client requests

Clients can ask to reschedule or cancel. Requests appear in To-dos and in Activity.

- For a reschedule request, check the new time and select **Move meeting**.
- For a cancellation request, select **Accept cancellation**. If it came in after your late cancellation window, it is recorded as a late cancellation.
- Select **Decline** to keep the meeting as it was.

You can add a reply for the client with either answer.

Once a meeting has started, its request can only be declined.

## Time off and extra hours

- **Time off**: clients can't pick times during it. Meetings already booked stay as they are, so move or cancel them yourself.
- **Extra hours**: clients can pick times during these hours as well as your usual week.

Notes on time off are visible to staff only. Clients just see fewer open times.
