Activity shows what your clients do, newest first: workouts, nutrition logs, meeting requests, service requests and payment notices. Items are grouped by day.

## Filters

<Example caption="Filters at the top of Activity." interactive>
  <div role="group" aria-label="Filter activity example" className="flex gap-2">
    <Chip pressed>All</Chip>
    <Chip pressed={false}>Focused</Chip>
    <Chip pressed={false}>Unread</Chip>
  </div>
</Example>

- **All**: everything.
- **Focused**: meeting requests, service requests and payment notices. These are the items you may need to act on. Answered ones stay here with their status.
- **Unread**: items you haven't opened yet.

## Opening an item

Select an item to open it where you can act on it:

- A **workout** opens the client's log on that week and day.
- A **nutrition log** opens the client's spreadsheet on that day.
- A **meeting request** opens the meeting on the client's page, with the request.
- A **service request** opens the client's page, where you can accept or reject it.
- A **payment notice** opens Billing, with Record payment ready for that notice.

Requests and payment notices also appear on the Meetings, Clients and Billing pages. You can answer them from there too.

## Unread items

The number beside Activity in the sidebar is how many unread items you have. Opening an item marks it as read. **Mark all as read** clears the whole list at once. Each staff member has their own read state.

Answered requests are no longer unread for anyone. Once one coach accepts or declines a request, it drops out of Unread for everyone.

## Labels

Labels on each item tell you what it is and what happened to it.

<Example caption="Some of the labels you'll see.">
  <Badge tone="info">Reschedule request</Badge>
  <Badge tone="danger">Cancellation request</Badge>
  <Badge tone="violet">Service Request</Badge>
  <Badge tone="gold">Payment</Badge>
  <Badge tone="gold">Short notice</Badge>
  <Badge tone="danger">Late cancellation</Badge>
  <Badge>Edited by coach</Badge>
</Example>

- **Short notice** and **Late cancellation** only apply to personal training meetings. They mean the request came in with less notice than the late cancellation window in Profile settings. If you accept a late cancellation, it is charged the session price. See [Billing](/coach/help/billing).
- **Edited by coach** marks a nutrition entry a coach corrected.
- Workouts show **Completed**, **In progress** or **Skipped**. A nutrition log with a value missing shows **Partial entry**.
- Requests show **Accepted**, **Declined**, **Withdrawn** or **Expired** once they are settled. An expired reschedule request was left unanswered until the meeting started.
- Payment notices show **Recorded**, **Dismissed** or **Paid** once you've dealt with them.

## Trends

**Trends** sits beside the list on a wide screen. It counts everything your clients sent, read or unread, over the range you choose:

- **Day**: today, by hour.
- **Week**: the last 7 days, by day.
- **Month**: the last 30 days, by day.

It has three parts:

- **Notifications received**: a column for each hour or day.
- **By type**: how many of each kind, such as completed workouts or nutrition logs.
- **By client**: the five busiest clients, with everyone else added together on one line. Select a name to open that client.

## One client's history

On a client's page, **Recent updates** shows their latest items. Select **View full history** to see everything for that client. You can also right-click their row on the Clients page and select **See recent updates**.

<Callout tone="tip">
On the Clients page, **To review** shows your latest unread items without opening Activity.
</Callout>
