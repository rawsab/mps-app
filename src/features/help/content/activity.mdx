Activity shows what your clients do, newest first: workouts, nutrition logs, meeting requests, service requests and payment notices.

## Filters

<Example caption="Filters at the top of Activity." interactive>
  <div role="group" aria-label="Filter activity example" className="flex gap-2">
    <Chip pressed>All</Chip>
    <Chip pressed={false}>Focused</Chip>
    <Chip pressed={false}>Unread</Chip>
  </div>
</Example>

- **All**: everything.
- **Focused**: requests that need an answer from you.
- **Unread**: items you haven't opened yet.

## Unread items

The number beside Activity in the sidebar is how many unread items you have. Opening an item marks it as read. **Mark all as read** clears the whole list at once.

## Labels

Labels on each item tell you what it is and what happened to it.

<Example caption="Some of the labels you'll see.">
  <Badge tone="info">Reschedule request</Badge>
  <Badge tone="danger">Cancellation request</Badge>
  <Badge tone="violet">Service Request</Badge>
  <Badge tone="gold">Payment</Badge>
  <Badge tone="gold">Short notice</Badge>
  <Badge tone="danger">Late cancellation</Badge>
</Example>

**Short notice** means a reschedule request came in close to the session. **Late cancellation** means a cancellation came in after the late cancellation window you set in Profile settings.

## Trends

**Trends** shows how many updates you received over a period you choose, split by type and by client.

## One client's history

On a client's page, **Recent updates** shows their latest items. Select **View full history** to see everything for that client.

<Callout tone="tip">
Requests and payment notices also appear on the Meetings and Billing pages, where you can act on them.
</Callout>
