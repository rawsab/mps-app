Open times are the times a client can pick when they ask to reschedule a meeting. The app works them out from your working hours, your time off and extra hours, your meetings and your booking rules.

Open times only limit what clients can pick. You can still add or move a meeting to any time yourself.

## Where to set it

- **Working hours** and **Booking rules**: the Availability card in Profile settings.
- **Time off** and **Extra hours**: the Time off & extra hours section on the Meetings page.

All of these are in your time zone. Clients see the open times in their own.

## Setting your working hours

1. Open Profile settings and find **Availability**.
2. Tick each day you work. A new day starts at 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
3. Change the start and end times.
4. To add a second range on a day, such as after lunch, select the + button at the end of its line. The trash button removes a range.
5. Select **Save availability**.

Ranges on the same day that overlap or touch are joined into one. A meeting can then run across them, such as 9 AM to 12 PM and 12 PM to 5 PM.

The line above the days sums up your week. If you have no working hours, clients can't pick new times. They can still send you a message saying what suits them.

If you change your time zone, your hours stay at the same clock times in the new zone.

## Booking rules

| Rule | What it does | Default | Allowed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Notice (hours)** | The soonest a client can pick, counted from now. | 12 | 0 to 168 |
| **Look ahead (days)** | How far ahead clients can pick. | 28 | 1 to 90 |
| **Gap (minutes)** | Time kept free before and after each meeting. | 0 | 0 to 120 |

Save them with **Save availability**, along with your hours.

## Time off and extra hours

On the Meetings page, find **Time off & extra hours**.

1. Select **Add time off** or **Add extra hours**.
2. For a whole day or several days, tick **All day** and choose the **First day** and **Last day**.
3. For part of a day, leave All day unticked and choose the **Date**, **From** and **To**.
4. Add a **Note** if you like. Only staff see it.
5. Select **Save**.

- **Time off** takes time away. Clients can't pick times during it.
- **Extra hours** add time outside your usual week, such as a Saturday morning.

To change or remove one, open its ⋯ menu and choose **Edit** or **Remove**.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Time off doesn't cancel meetings">
Meetings already booked during time off stay scheduled. When you save, the app lists them so you can move or cancel each one.
</Callout>

## How open times are worked out

For each day, the app takes:

1. Your working hours for that weekday.
2. Plus any extra hours.
3. Minus any time off. Time off wins where it overlaps extra hours.
4. Minus your scheduled meetings, with the gap kept free on each side.
5. Minus times other clients have asked for and are waiting on, with the gap on each side.
6. Minus anything sooner than your notice, or further ahead than your look ahead.

A meeting can then start every 15 minutes, on the quarter hour, wherever its whole length fits. A longer meeting has fewer start times. The illustrative diagram below shows how this works.

When a client asks to move a meeting, its current time still counts as free. They can pick a new time that overlaps it, such as 30 minutes later.

## Held times

When a client picks a time, it's held until you answer the request, they withdraw it, or the meeting starts. Other clients can't pick a held time.

If two clients choose the same time at once, the second is told that the time is no longer available, and the open times refresh.

## On the calendar

In week and day views on the Meetings page, time outside your working hours and extra hours is striped. In month view, days with time off say Time off.

## Short notice

Your **Late cancellation window** is in Profile settings, under Personal training sessions. It doesn't change open times. A personal training client who asks to cancel or reschedule inside it sees a Short notice warning first:

- An accepted cancellation is recorded as a late cancellation.
- A reschedule request shows as Short notice to you. If you accept it, nothing is recorded as late.
