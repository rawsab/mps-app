Billing tracks what each client owes and what they have paid. Amounts are in Canadian dollars, before tax. Clients pay you by e-Transfer outside the app, and you record each payment here.

## How charges are made

- **Monthly services** (Mesocycle and Nutrition) are charged on the 1st of each month.
- **Personal training sessions** are charged when you record attendance.
- **No-shows and late cancellations** are charged the full session price. You can void the charge if you choose not to bill it.
- Meetings that aren't personal training, such as check-ins, are never charged. The monthly price covers them.

## Session prices

A session's price comes from the first of these that is set:

1. A price set on that meeting.
2. The client's own price for that kind of session.
3. The default price.

Setting a price on a meeting also makes it that client's price for that kind of session from then on.

A price of $0 is complimentary. A missing price is different: the session isn't charged until you set one. Sessions like this are listed on the Billing page.

<Callout tone="note">
Administrators set the default prices with **Default prices** on the Billing page. Changes apply to new charges only.
</Callout>

## The Billing page

The Billing page lists your clients with their balances:

- **Outstanding**: what is owed now.
- **From previous months**: unpaid amounts from earlier months.
- **Overdue** and **In credit** help you find clients who are behind or paid ahead.

## When a client says they've paid

When a client sends an e-Transfer, they can tell you in their app. A notice appears on the Billing page and in Activity.

1. Check that the e-Transfer arrived.
2. Select **Record payment** and enter the amount and date received.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Balances only change when you record a payment">
A client's notice doesn't change their balance. Record the payment once the money arrives.
</Callout>

- **Record payment**: money received. It pays the oldest unpaid charges first. Anything left over becomes credit for later charges.
- **Add a charge**: a one-off amount, such as an opening balance.
- **Add a credit**: lowers the balance without counting as money received, for goodwill or a discount after the fact.
- **Mark paid** and **Mark unpaid**: settle a month, or undo one marked paid by mistake.
- **Void**: cancels a charge or credit. It no longer counts toward the balance, but stays on record.
- **Edit client's prices**: this client's own prices for monthly services and sessions.

**Statements** shows each earlier month. **Payments** lists every payment recorded.

Notes on payments and credits are private to staff.
