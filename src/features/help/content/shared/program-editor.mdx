## Drafts and publishing

Every program starts as a draft. Drafts are visible to staff only.

<Example caption="Labels you'll see on programs.">
  <Badge tone="gold">Live</Badge>
  <Badge>Not published</Badge>
  <Badge tone="gold">Current</Badge>
  <Badge>Archived</Badge>
</Example>

1. Build the program. Changes save on their own about a second after you stop.
2. Open **Publish readiness** beside the Publish button. It lists anything you need to fix, such as an exercise with no name. Select an item to jump to it.
3. Select **Publish**. The draft becomes the client's current program for this service.

Publishing again later replaces what the client sees with your new version. Anything the client has already logged is kept.

<Callout tone="note">
If Publish is refused, you may not have permission to publish for this client. Ask an administrator.
</Callout>

## Two views

<Example caption="Switch between the two views at the top of the editor." interactive width="sm">
  <SegmentedControl label="Editor view example" value="grid" onChange={() => {}} options={[{ value: 'grid', label: 'Spreadsheet' }, { value: 'program', label: 'List View' }]} />
</Example>

Both views edit the same draft, so you can switch at any time.

### Spreadsheet

The Spreadsheet shows each week as a table, one row per exercise. It opens by default.

- Type in a cell to change it. Paste a block of cells from another spreadsheet, and you'll be asked to review the change first.
- Drag a day, section or exercise to reorder it. Shift-click or Shift-drag to select several cells.
- Right-click a cell to duplicate or remove its day or week.
- Use **Add** to create weeks, days and sections.
- The **+** bar under the table adds an exercise to the last section.

### List View

List View shows one workout at a time, with an outline of weeks and days on the side. Use it for things the spreadsheet doesn't show: program details, every-workout prep, resources, exercise alternatives, demo links and the muscles each exercise works.

## Writing targets

| You type | It means |
| --- | --- |
| `10` | 10 reps on every set |
| `10 / 8 / 6` | A different target for each set |
| `8-10` | A range of 8 to 10 reps |
| `45s` or `2 min` | A time instead of reps |
| `1-2` in RIR | A range of 1 to 2 reps in reserve |

A load with no unit stays without one. The app never guesses kilograms or pounds. A blank cell means no value. It is different from 0.

## Undo and redo

Use the undo and redo buttons, or press **Ctrl+Z** (**⌘Z** on a Mac) to undo and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** (**⌘⇧Z**) to redo. Undo covers your last 100 changes while the editor is open.

## When something goes wrong

- **A save fails**: your edits stay in the editor. Select **Retry**, or keep editing and it tries again.
- **Someone else changed the draft**: nothing is overwritten. You choose whether to add your changes on top of theirs, replace theirs or discard yours.
- **You close the page with unsaved edits**: a copy stays on this device. You'll be offered it the next time you open the program.

## Current and archived programs

A client can have several programs. **Current** marks the one they see now. Use **Make current** to show a different program instead.

Archived programs keep their history, and they can't be edited or published.
