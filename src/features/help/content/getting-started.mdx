This workspace is where you run your coaching. You manage clients, build their programs, schedule sessions and track payments here. Clients use a separate app to see what you publish and to log their results.

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists the main areas:

- **Clients**: everyone you coach, plus invitations and requests to follow up. Open the arrow beside it to jump straight to an active client.
- **Meetings**: your calendar, requests to move or cancel sessions, and attendance.
- **Billing**: what each client owes, payments to record and prices.
- **Templates**: reusable programs. This area is still being built.
- **Activity**: workouts, nutrition logs and requests from your clients.

A number beside an item shows what needs you: unread activity, meetings left today or payments to check.

**Help** and your **Profile settings** sit at the bottom. The buttons below them switch between light and dark mode, sign you out and collapse the sidebar. On a phone, open the sidebar with the menu button at the top left.

## Search

Search finds clients, programs, pages and settings. Open it with the search button at the top of the sidebar, or press <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd><span className="sr-only">Command K</span> on a Mac or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd><span className="sr-only">Control K</span> on Windows.

Type a few letters and use the arrow keys to pick a result. Press Enter to open it. Search understands everyday words, so "vacation" finds Time off and "payments" finds Billing.

## Saving

Most changes save on their own. Program drafts save about a second after you stop typing, and a label near the top shows when they are saved. Forms with a Save button only save when you press it.

<Callout tone="tip" title="Drafts stay private">
Clients only see a program after you publish it. You can edit a draft as much as you like first.
</Callout>

## Getting help

Open **Help** from the sidebar at any time. Use the search box at the top of Help to find a topic by its name or by words in it.
