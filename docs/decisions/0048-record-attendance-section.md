# 0048 — Record attendance on the Meetings page

Date: 2026-11-20 · Status: implemented (owner request), manual UI verification pending · Extends 0047

- **Owner request:** a fast, accessible way to record attendance without opening each meeting. Owner choices: newest first, a "Mark all completed" bulk action, attendance moved out of the to-dos, and no default attendance (an ended meeting stays "Not recorded" until someone records it).
- **Layout:** the Meetings page's to-dos row now pairs **To-dos** (pending meeting requests) with **Record attendance**, side by side on large screens like Next meeting and Upcoming. A side with nothing in it gives the other the full width; with neither, the row folds away as before. The requests became a vertical list (4, then "Show all"), since a sideways strip doesn't fit half a column. The Clients page's service-request strip is unchanged.
- **Record attendance:** ended meetings without attendance (`list_meetings_needing_attendance`), grouped by day in the coach's time zone (Today, Yesterday, then dates), newest first.
  - Each row shows the day and start time (Today, Yesterday, then dates) above the client's name and services (services hidden on phones), and opens the meeting. Rows are separated by divider lines, with no surrounding box (owner request). Then two icon buttons, a red ✕ (No-show) and a green ✓ (Mark completed), with no fill or border until hovered (owner request). Eight rows show, then "Show all".
  - A recorded row leaves at once, and a notice offers **Undo**, which is a logged correction back to "not recorded".
  - Once shown, the section stays for the visit, so recording the last meeting keeps its Undo.
- **Accessibility:** buttons name their meeting ("Mark Grace Lee, Tue, Nov 17 7:00 AM completed"). After a row is recorded, focus moves to the same button on the next row, or to the heading when none are left. The notice is a polite live region.
- **Bulk (migration 00047):** "Mark all completed" confirms with the count, then calls `record_attendance_many(ids, status)`. It's all or nothing: every meeting must be accessible, started, unrecorded and without a pending request, or nothing is recorded. Meetings are locked in id order, and each is audited like `record_attendance`. Up to 200 per call.
- **Calendar preview:** a started, unrecorded meeting's preview has No-Show and Mark Completed beside Edit meeting (also owner request).
- Grouping and focus order are pure functions in `src/domain/scheduling/attendance.ts`.
- **Verification:** `036_record_attendance_many.test.sql` (9 pgTAP assertions); the full suite passes (894). `tests/domain/meetingAttendance.test.ts`; `pnpm verify` passes with 335 unit tests. No browser checks.
- **Meetings open in place (owner request):** on the Meetings page, every meeting link (calendar preview's Edit meeting, Next meeting, Upcoming, To-dos, Record attendance) now opens the full meeting dialog on the page itself (`?meeting=`), with the calendar's view and date kept behind it. Before, they went to the client's page with `?session=`. The client's name in the dialog links to their page. "Change the repeating pattern" opens the pattern editor in place. This supersedes 0040's "clicking a meeting opens it on the client's page".
- **Motion (owner feedback):** a recorded row fades out (180ms), then leaves the list, and the rows below slide up into its place (260ms). The notice appears only after that. Undo reverses it: the notice folds away, the rows below slide down, and the meeting fades back in. Animating row height stuttered, so only opacity is animated, and the slide uses transforms measured before and after the change (FLIP, Web Animations API). With reduced motion, rows just appear and disappear.
- **Browser-checked motion (owner approved, 2026-11-20):** recorded frame by frame in headless Chrome. Two bugs were found and fixed. The save's refetch dropped a row for a few frames before its fade began, so rows being saved now stay drawn. The notice pushed the list down after the slide, so it now floats at the bottom of the screen, portalled to `<body>` because the route's entrance transform would otherwise pin `fixed` to the page content. The slide is done with `flushSync`, so measuring, updating and starting the slide happen before the next paint. Result: the row fades, then the rows below glide up in one movement, then the notice appears at the bottom.
